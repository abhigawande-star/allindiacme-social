## A. Fixed brand rules — founder only

No worker edits this section. The Standards Researcher may propose a change in section C.
*Restored 22 Sep 2026 word for word from the founder-set rules recorded in
`claude/social-media-strategy.md` §4 and the safety rules in `claude/social-content-machine.md`.*

1. **Clinical accuracy outranks engagement.** A dose, caveat or contraindication is never cut to
   make a line read better. Never sacrifice medical credibility for reach.
2. **CME first; masterclass is the format, not the product** (founder, 22 Sep 2026). What All
   India CME offers is CME. The masterclass is how that CME is taught: longitudinal, one skill,
   twenty-four sessions a year. Say "CME in masterclass format", "a year-long CME programme",
   "CME, taught the masterclass way", "a CME masterclass". Never present "masterclass" as the
   product, never set masterclasses against CME ("masterclasses and cross-specialty CME"), and
   never describe the masterclass without saying it is CME.
3. Sentence case everywhere. **No emoji, ever.**
4. Exactly three hashtags, always `#CME #MedEd` plus one theme tag, after a blank line at the end
   of the caption. No hashtags inside the body.
5. No claim without a number behind it, and no number without a source. No invented statistics.
6. Every image carries the lockup, the tagline *Uniform Excellence in Clinical Practice* and
   *A venture of Covelis Health Tech LLP.* (render.py does this).
7. Grid rhythm navy → ivory → gold across consecutive slots; gold never next to gold.
8. **Not until the founder confirms them:** no CME dates, faculty names, prices, accreditation or
   credit claims, partner names, and no CME promotion posts.
9. Every clinical claim is tied to a named, current guideline that both the Factory and the
   Guardian opened. Indian body first (IAP, FOGSI, RSSDI, ICMR, ISA, ISCCM, NNF), else WHO or the
   leading international guideline. Generic drug names only.
10. Vignettes are invented and generic. No patient-identifiable detail, however anonymised.
11. Respect protocol (Architecture §12): professional, courteous, never shaming, never
    manipulative, no pressure.

## B. Strategy — the Standards Researcher edits, with date and source

- **Cadence:** 2 posts a day, 09:30 and 17:30 IST. Both slots sit on the strongest windows in
  Buffer's 9.6M-post analysis (9 a.m. and 6 p.m. local time); keep them.
- **Day weighting (24 Sep 2026):** Wednesday and Thursday are the strongest days, Friday and
  Saturday the weakest (Buffer, 9.6M posts, Jan 2024 – Dec 2025). Volume does not change; placement
  does. Put the week's strongest clinical post or carousel on Wednesday or Thursday, and put brand
  and lighter educational posts on Friday and Saturday.
- **Mix (share of output):** clinical knowledge 40%, educational 25%, brand 20%, CME promotion 10%
  (paused until A8 clears — use clinical or educational instead), post-CME 5% (none yet).
  AYUSH-relevant posts sit inside these pillars; aim for about one in seven.
- **Rotation:** no topic or headline repeated within the last 60 posts. Rotate specialties:
  obstetrics and gynaecology, anaesthesia and pain, paediatrics and neonatology, diabetes and
  internal medicine, cardiology, orthopaedics, radiology, AYUSH.
- **Clinical post shape:** one actionable point, the caveat that matters, source on the image or
  in the caption.
- **The shape that earns shares (24 Sep 2026):** in the one published analysis of a
  clinician-facing medical-education account, educational posts — board-review cases, clinical
  infographics, annotated images — drew the most shares, while people-and-milestone posts drew the
  most likes and comments. We optimise for shares, so build the clinical post as a case or a
  question with the answer and the pearl, not as a statement.
- **Formats from 9 Oct 2026** (revised 24 Sep 2026; old rule and evidence in section C): per week
  6 single squares, 4 carousels (2 reference, ≤10 slides, and 2 MCQ question), and 2 Reels once a
  Reels route exists. Never two carousels back to back; not on CME day or the day before.
  Carousels need the Publisher's carousel flow and Reels need a video route. Until each is tested,
  draft single squares only **and record the unmet slot** — a missing carousel or Reel is a
  capability gap to escalate, not a slot to fill silently.
- **Optimise for** shares and saves, not likes. The confirmed ranking signals are watch time,
  likes per reach and sends per reach; a send is weighted several times a like and a save about
  three times a like.
- **Compliance constraints (24 Sep 2026; NMC social-media guidance for registered medical
  practitioners, 13 Aug 2023):** no patient photographs or scan images; no before-and-after,
  healed-patient, surgery or procedure imagery, however impressive the result; nothing that
  solicits patients — every post is clinician-facing; never buy likes, followers or ranking.
  These sit alongside A10 and A11 and bind the Factory and the Guardian equally.
- **Reality check (24 Sep 2026):** measured reach is 2–8 accounts per post, with zero saves and
  zero shares on every post old enough to measure. Format tuning cannot fix a distribution problem
  of that size. Until reach is consistently in the hundreds, treat these format rules as
  preparation, and name distribution — not format — as the binding constraint in each Guardian run.

## C. Research log — dated entries, newest first

- 24 Sep 2026 — **Platform scan, 25 Aug – 24 Sep 2026: no ranking-signal change announced in the
  window.** Three items, none requiring a rule change. (1) From 31 Aug 2026 Instagram labels
  AI-generated profiles and removes unlabelled ones from recommendations; the scope is synthetic
  personas, not accounts that use AI tools in production, so our machine-rendered cards and
  drafted captions are outside it — no action. (2) 10 Sep 2026, Mosseri defended ranked feed
  against a chronological mandate; a statement, not a change. (3) 15 Sep 2026, Meta One Links put
  clickable links in organic posts behind a paid tier — a watch item for registration links once
  A8 clears, nothing to do now. Sources:
  https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/ and
  https://www.netinfluencer.com/instagram-to-limit-the-reach-of-unlabeled-ai-generated-profiles/
- 24 Sep 2026 — **Weekly format rule changed.** Old: "per week 10 single squares, 2 reference
  carousels (≤10 slides), 2 MCQ question carousels". New: 6 single squares, 4 carousels, 2 Reels
  once a Reels route exists. Evidence: single-image reach fell 21.96% year on year, interactions
  25.41% and engagement 45.98%, while carousels produce nine times the saves of a single image and
  Reels more than four times the interactions (Metricool, 24,364,803 posts across 375,118 accounts,
  Jan–Feb 2025 against Jan–Feb 2026). For accounts under 50K followers Reels lead median reach,
  and carousels take over as the account grows (Metricool/HypeAuditor Instagram Content Playbook,
  up to 70M posts). To reverse, restore the old line. Sources:
  https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/ and
  https://liahaberman.substack.com/p/the-best-instagram-content-formats
- 24 Sep 2026 — **Day weighting added** (new rule, nothing replaced): Wednesday and Thursday
  strongest, Friday and Saturday weakest; 9 a.m., 12 p.m. and 6 p.m. local the strongest hours,
  which is where our 09:30 and 17:30 IST slots already sit. Buffer, 9.6M posts from 200,000+
  accounts, Jan 2024 – Dec 2025, normalised to local time. To reverse, delete the bullet.
  Source: https://buffer.com/resources/when-is-the-best-time-to-post-on-instagram/
- 24 Sep 2026 — **"The shape that earns shares" added** (new rule, nothing replaced). A 90-day
  analysis of an academic infectious-diseases fellowship account (1,053 followers, 90 days to
  24 Jul 2025, 50,015 views, 4,560 unique accounts reached) found that educational content —
  board-review cases, infographics, clinical images — drew the most shares, while faculty and
  milestone posts drew the most likes and comments: "while users interact with personal content,
  they share educational content". To reverse, delete the bullet. Source:
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12515378/
- 24 Sep 2026 — **"Optimise for" line extended.** Old: "Optimise for shares and saves, not likes."
  New: the same, plus the confirmed signals — watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach — and
  their rough weights. This confirms the existing rule rather than changing it. Source:
  https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026/
- 24 Sep 2026 — **Compliance constraints added** (new rule, nothing replaced), from the NMC
  social-media guidance for registered medical practitioners dated 13 Aug 2023: no patient
  photographs or scan images (rule 3); no images of healed patients or surgery/procedure videos
  "displaying impressive results under any circumstances" (rule 7); soliciting patients directly
  or indirectly is unethical (rule 11); no purchasing likes, followers or ranking (rule 5). Still
  the operative guidance. Source:
  https://medicaldialogues.in/health-news/nmc/how-to-behave-on-social-media-nmc-releases-11-commandments-for-doctors-115773
- 24 Sep 2026 — **Our own performance.** Nine posts live; five old enough to measure. Reach 2–8
  accounts each, zero saves, zero shares, zero comments throughout, one profile visit in total.
  Best: 7 Sep, "the future of healthcare is evolving" — reach 8, 3 likes. Also 21 Sep, the
  Gadchiroli sepsis brand post — reach 7, 2 likes, the single profile visit. Worst: 11 and 12 Sep,
  the pre-rebuild emoji-and-fifteen-hashtag posts — reach 2 each. No pillar or specialty can be
  told apart at this volume, and no conclusion about format should be drawn from it. What the
  numbers do say is that the account has no distribution yet. Recorded as the "reality check"
  bullet in section B.
- 24 Sep 2026 — **Proposal for the founder (section A4, hashtags).** A4 fixes exactly three
  hashtags. Across 1.6M posts, five hashtags gave the best reach and the best engagement; one to
  three performed *worse than none at all*; more than five performed worst. Mosseri has separately
  said hashtags no longer directly affect reach, so the effect is small but it is measurable and
  it currently runs against us. Proposal: allow five — keep `#CME #MedEd` and add three theme tags
  rather than one. No change made; A4 stands until the founder decides. Source:
  https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/do-instagram-hashtags-work/
- 24 Sep 2026 — **Proposal for the founder (distribution).** At 2–8 accounts reached per post, the
  content is not the constraint. The fellowship-account study above attributes reach growth largely
  to faculty and trainees resharing posts. Seeding — founder and confirmed faculty resharing to
  their own networks, and the doctor WhatsApp route — sits outside this rulebook and needs a
  founder decision. Flagged, not actioned.
